## Project

**ResearchVault**

ResearchVault is a modern semantic research discovery platform and academic workspace designed for students, researchers, academics, and developers. It allows users to discover relevant research papers and academic literature using natural-language conceptual queries, upload their own PDFs/documents, search across passages, filter with academic metadata, preview documents, and organize research into libraries and collections.

**Core Value:** Semantic research discovery that understands conceptual intent rather than relying solely on exact keywords, delivering accurate passage retrieval and trustworthy academic metadata without mock data.

### Constraints

- **Tech stack**: Next.js (TypeScript) frontend + FastAPI (Python) backend + PostgreSQL + ChromaDB.
- **Design Tokens**: Strict adherence to light color palette (#315EAB accent, #202733 text) and typography (Poppins headings, Verdana body, Roboto metadata).
- **Integrity**: Zero mock/fabricated papers or synthetic scores; all returned passages and relevance scores must be genuine.
- **Performance**: Asynchronous document processing, batched embedding generation, server-side pagination.
